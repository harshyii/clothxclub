& refunds.

Eligible purchases may be exchanged within 365 days, subject to the conditions on this page. Contact CLOTH X CLUB if an item is damaged, defective, incorrect or if you need help with an exchange request.

Before making a purchase

Please check the size, colour, fit and condition of an item before purchase. Eligible items may be exchanged within 365 days from the date of purchase, subject to the conditions below and presentation of the original bill or proof of purchase.

How to request help

  1. Contact us at +91 99915 03210 or clothxclub@gmail.com.
  2. Share your bill or proof of purchase and explain the issue.
  3. Wait for our team to confirm the next step before sending or leaving an item with us.

365-day exchange window

Eligible items may be exchanged within 365 days from the purchase date. The exchange period does not mean that every item is automatically eligible. Items must meet the applicable conditions, including being unused, unwashed, undamaged and accompanied by their original tags and proof of purchase. Items showing wear, alteration, damage after purchase or missing tags may be refused. Certain products, clearance items or special offers may have separate conditions; please check the applicable terms at the time of purchase.

Exchanges are subject to inspection and availability of the requested replacement. If the same item, size or colour is unavailable, our team will explain the available exchange options.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If you believe an item supplied by us is damaged, defective or different from what you purchased, contact us promptly with details and photographs where possible. We will review the issue and explain the available remedy in accordance with applicable consumer law.

Refunds and exchanges

Our standard arrangement is exchange only; we do not offer refunds for change of mind, incorrect size selection or preference changes. If an item is verified as defective or incorrectly supplied, we will review the issue and provide an appropriate remedy, including a refund where required by applicable consumer law. Any refund that is approved will be processed using the original payment method where reasonably possible, or another method agreed with the customer.

Online orders and shipping

Online checkout and shipping are not currently enabled on this website. This policy currently relates to enquiries and purchases arranged directly with the store. We will publish the applicable delivery, cancellation and return procedure before enabling online orders.
